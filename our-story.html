● OUR STORY

From a simple idea. To something bigger.

Johar Hub didn't begin as a big business plan. It started with a simple idea — and slowly became a place where different interests, creative projects and ideas could exist under one identity.

01
THE BEGINNING

It started with a simple shop.

The idea behind Johar Hub first appeared around late 2024 and developed further in 2025.

At the beginning, the vision was much simpler: a physical shop providing document services, online work, stationery and other everyday digital services.

There was no bigger ecosystem planned at the time. It was simply the idea of building something of my own.

02
THE NAME

From Johar Digitals to Johar Hub.

The original idea was called Johar Digitals.

But over time, different interests started coming together — fashion, creative work, technology, community projects and other ideas that didn't really fit under a single digital-service shop.

That's when the idea of Johar Hub took shape: an umbrella where different ventures and creative directions could grow together.

JOHAR

A greeting. A simple word of connection and respect.

ROOTED IN IDENTITY

Built from where I come from.

Adivasi identity became a natural part of Johar Hub because its creator comes from an Adivasi community.

What started as personal identity gradually became a creative direction — the desire to build an Adivasi-inspired brand and bring that identity into modern products, design and creative work.
