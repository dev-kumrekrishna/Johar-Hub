● OUR STORY

From a simple idea. To something bigger.

Johar Hub began with a simple digital-workspace idea. What started as Johar Cyber Cafe gradually evolved into a broader vision — bringing technology, fashion, creativity and community projects under one identity.

01
THE BEGINNING

It started with Johar Cyber Cafe.

The earliest version of the idea was called Johar Cyber Cafe. The vision was to create a digital workspace where people could access computers, internet and everyday digital services.

It was imagined as a practical local workspace — a place for online forms, document work, printing-related needs, internet access and other useful digital services. The goal was not just to run a shop, but to build something useful that people could rely on.

At that stage, the idea was focused on one physical space and the services it could offer. The larger Johar Hub ecosystem had not taken shape yet; the first goal was simply to create an independent workspace of my own.

02
THE NAME

From Cyber Cafe to Johar Hub.

As the idea developed, the name and direction evolved from Johar Cyber Cafe to Johar Digitals — a name that put more emphasis on digital services and technology.

Over time, my interests grew beyond a digital-service workspace. Fashion, design, technology, creative work and community projects all became part of the bigger picture. They needed room to develop in their own ways.

That is when Johar Hub took shape as the umbrella identity — a home for different ventures and creative directions, without forcing every project to use the same branch name.

JOHAR

A greeting. A simple word of connection and respect.

ROOTED IN IDENTITY

Built from where I come from.

Adivasi identity became a natural part of Johar Hub because its creator comes from an Adivasi community.

What started as personal identity gradually became a creative direction — the desire to build an Adivasi-inspired brand and bring that identity into modern products, design and creative work.
