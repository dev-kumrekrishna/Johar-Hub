OUR STORY

From a simple idea.
To something bigger.

Johar Hub began with a simple digital-workspace idea. What started as Johar Cyber Cafe gradually evolved into a broader vision bringing technology, fashion, creativity and community projects under one identity.

01. THE BEGINNING

It started with Johar Cyber Cafe.

The earliest version of the idea was called Johar Cyber Cafe. The vision was to create a digital workspace where people could access computers, internet and everyday digital services.

It was imagined as a practical local workspace. The goal was not just to run a shop, but to build something useful that people could rely on before the larger ecosystem took shape.

02. THE EVOLUTION

From Cyber Cafe to Johar Hub.

As the idea developed, the direction evolved into Johar Digitals. Over time, my interests grew beyond a digital-service workspace. Fashion, design, technology, and community projects needed room to develop.

That is when Johar Hub took shape as the umbrella identity a home for different ventures and creative directions.

JOHAR

A greeting. A simple word of connection and respect.

03. ROOTED IN IDENTITY

Built from where I come from.

Adivasi identity became a natural part of Johar Hub because its creator comes from an Adivasi community. What started as personal identity gradually became a creative direction.

Identity Culture Creativity Community

No grand turning point.
Just an obsession.

There wasn't one dramatic moment that created Johar Hub. Just a persistent obsession with building something of my own something creative, independent and rooted in where I come from.
